Rekura

Privacy Policy · Effective August 15, 2026

Rekura is an app for learning English vocabulary. It is fully usable without an account: in that case all your data — cards, translations, notes, images, review history — is stored only on your device, and we have no access to it.

What we collect and why

Account and sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google to sync your library between devices, the cloud (Supabase, servers in the EU) stores: your sign-in email address, your cards — words, translations, transcriptions, notes, examples — attached images, sets, and review history with retention metrics. This data serves one purpose: showing your library on your other devices. Only you can access it — rows and files are isolated per account at the database level.

Requests to external services

  • Card autofill: the word you type is sent to a dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev / Wiktionary) to fetch its transcription and usage examples.
  • Image search (signed-in users only): the word is sent through our server to the Pixabay and Pexels photo services. Requests are limited by a daily quota; nothing but the word itself is shared.

Word pronunciation uses the system's own voices, on the device; no text is sent anywhere for speech.

Diagnostics (can be turned off)

The app sends crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and anonymous usage statistics of screens and features (Firebase Analytics). These events contain no words, no translations, none of your card content, and are not linked to your account. No advertising identifier is collected. The toggle is in Settings → About; it disables both crash reports and usage statistics at once.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no data collection for advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Your cards are used for nothing but syncing between your own devices.

Storage and deletion

Cloud data is kept as long as the account exists. You can delete the account right in the app (Settings → Sync → “Delete account”) — this permanently erases all cloud data, including the account itself. To delete the account without the app, see the account deletion page. On-device data can be erased in the same settings (“Erase all data”) or by uninstalling the app.

Your rights

At any time you can export all your data to a file (Settings → “Export”), correct it in the app, delete it or the whole account, and turn diagnostics off. For any questions about your data, write to 49serggor@gmail.com — we will respond and fulfil requests within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
